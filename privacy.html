SubWatch Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

What SubWatch is

SubWatch is a free, open, single-page web app that finds your subscriptions by scanning your Gmail for receipts and reminds you before they renew. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no SubWatch server, no backend, and no database.

What data SubWatch accesses

SubWatch asks for two Google permissions:

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): used only to search your mailbox for receipt, invoice, and payment emails and read those messages so SubWatch can detect the service name, amount, currency, and billing cycle.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): used only when you tap "Remind me", to create and delete reminder events on your own calendar.

SubWatch does not read emails unrelated to receipts beyond what the receipt search returns, and it never sends, deletes, or modifies your email.

Where your data goes

Nowhere except between your browser and Google's APIs. All extracted subscription data is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is transmitted to, stored on, or processed by any SubWatch server or third party - no analytics, no ads, no trackers, no data sale, ever.

How Google data is used

SubWatch's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the subscription tracking and reminder features you see in the app. It is not used for advertising, not used to train models, not shared with anyone, and not accessed by any human.

How to delete your data

All SubWatch data lives in your browser. To delete everything, clear the site data for this page in your browser settings, or use your browser's "clear storage" option for this site. Reminder events SubWatch created live in your own Google Calendar and can be deleted there. You can revoke SubWatch's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
